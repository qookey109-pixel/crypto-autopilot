# SHORT Score Calibration V0.1

The directional challenger already produces a SHORT technical score. This
protocol defines the missing path from that paper score to reviewable evidence
without claiming a formal SHORT SState score.

Required evidence includes:

- at least 100 out-of-sample SHORT trades;
- at least 30 SHORT trades per regime;
- score calibration reported with Brier, log loss, ECE and MCE;
- funding-cost stress;
- time-aligned basis and open-interest context when verifiable;
- a dedicated short-squeeze regime; and
- asset-specific session/borrow assumptions for tokenized candidates.

Formal LONG weights may not be mirrored into SHORT. A PASS means SHORT score
evidence is ready for human review only and grants no formal SHORT or trading
authority.
